The Seam

Every path through this work crosses one joint: the crossing from what this chain is to what its governance owes. That crossing is real. It is not a deduction, and it is not hidden. These pages hold it in one place, argue for holding it that way, and audit whether the rest of the corpus keeps its promise to mark it.
