Foundations

  • FoundationsWhat Cardano IsThe short form of the whole position: what I hold this chain to be, what that commits me to, the three readings I do not hold, and the four things that would change my mind.

  • FoundationsFrom Coordination Protocol to Constitutional CommonsThe lower-friction entrance. Translates the ontology into the blockchain context and explains why a holder is not an investor, a user, or a voter.
  • FoundationsThe Rights of Participants in a Constitutional Coordination CommonsSix rights, each traced from the foundational commitment to the specific abrogation path that severs it. This is the specification of what the governance-layer sensor detects.
  • FoundationsThe Transaction as Constitutional MomentTurns “here are the rights” into “here are the conditions under which the consent the commons rests on stays genuine.” The fee-consent branch of the derivation.

  • FoundationsPlace-Severance: A Derivation NoteA finding that belongs beneath the foundational right rather than beside the six: the place axis of identity. A derivation note, not a seventh right.

The Seam

  • The SeamThe Half We Haven't Built YetStart here if you are new. Cardano shipped a transaction that proves its own correctness with no one you have to trust. There is a second half to that achievement, one layer up, still unbuilt.
  • The SeamThe Verification GapConsensus proves procedure, and at the consensus layer procedure is legitimacy. One layer up it is not — so the same proof verifies the wrong invariant. A verifiably valid vote can be an illegitimate one.
  • The SeamWhat the Second Sensor ReadsThe instrument was named and left empty on purpose. This is what fills it — and why the conditions are discovered rather than decreed.
  • The SeamThe Legibility of the SeamThe third stance, between uninterrogable certainty and uninterrogable uncertainty. The epistemic commitment that obliges every document here to carry the question alongside the conclusion.
